Our story

A corner of the village built for people who'd rather be outside.

Outside In started with a simple idea: bring the gear we love from the mountains and the coast to the town we call home. Walk in and you'll find honest advice, a well-edited wall of layers, and a shop that feels a little like a base camp for the South Shore.
