Our Story

Born from a love of the outdoors.

"Play hard outside — feel good inside."

A few years in Jackson, Wyoming barely scratched the outdoor itch. The more time spent on the mountain, the river, or the trail, the more it became clear: the right gear changes everything.

Outside In was born from that passion — and a desire to bring it home to the South Shore. Seeing you run by or cycle through town still brings a smile. We're here to outfit your next adventure, big or small.

We're more than a shop. We're a hub — a place to get fitted, find your next race, join a training group, and talk to people who actually do this stuff.
