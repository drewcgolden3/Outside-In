Our story

Born from a love of the outdoors.

A few years in Jackson, Wyoming barely scratched the outdoor itch. The more time spent on the mountain, the river, or the trail, the more it became clear: the right gear changes everything.

Outside In was born from that passion, and a desire to bring it home to the South Shore.
