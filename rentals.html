How it works

No app, no kiosk, just a quick call and a friendly handoff at the counter.

Good to know

Rental questions, answered.

Do I need to reserve in advance?

We recommend it. Bikes are limited and go quickly on weekends and sunny days. A quick call to (781) 383-0346 is the best way to make sure one's waiting for you.

How long is a rental?

Rentals are by the day, so you've got from pickup until closing to explore. Just have the bike back before we close for the evening.

Where's a good place to ride?

From the shop you can reach Cohasset Harbor, the village common and Sandy Beach with an easy ride. Tell us what you're up for and we'll sketch out a route when you pick up.

Do you rent paddleboards too?

Rentals are currently bikes only. We do carry paddleboards and water gear for sale in the shop, though, so stop in or give us a call if you're looking to gear up for the season.
