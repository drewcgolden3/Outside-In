How it started

The outdoors start at your front door.

Outside In grew out of a love for the coast and the mountains, and a wish that the gear we relied on lived a little closer to home. So we opened a shop in the middle of Cohasset village, filled it with the brands we trust, and left the door open (the orange one, you can't miss it).

What you get is a boutique that feels personal. We know our inventory because we chose every piece of it. We can tell you which shell actually breathes, which sock survives a thru-hike, and which fleece you'll never take off. It's retail the way it used to be, and, we think, the way it should be.

What we believe

Play hard outside. Feel good inside.

It's on our logo for a reason. It's how we choose everything on the wall.

Gear that goes the distance

We stock brands built to be repaired and re-worn, because the most sustainable jacket is the one you already own.

Proud to be local

We're a Main Street shop in a village we love, part of the fabric of the South Shore, not a checkout page in a warehouse.

Advice, not upselling

Tell us where you're headed and we'll get you the right kit, nothing you don't need, and nothing you'll regret.

We seek to be a hub where not only can you get outfitted for your next adventure, but you can join a training group, check out upcoming races on our info board, or ask questions of our knowledgeable staff. We're In to help you get Outside, because we know…Play hard outside, feel good inside.
