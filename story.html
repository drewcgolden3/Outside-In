Our story

How it started

The right gear changes everything.

A few years in Jackson, Wyoming barely scratched the outdoor itch. The more time spent on the mountain, the river, or the trail, the more it became clear: the right gear changes everything.

Outside In was born from that passion, and a desire to bring it home to the South Shore. Seeing you run by or cycle through town still brings a smile.

We're more than a shop. We're a hub, a place to get fitted, find your next race, join a training group, and talk to people who actually do this stuff.

"Play hard outside. Feel good inside." It's not just a tagline on the sign — it's how we think about every piece of gear we put on the shelves.

What we're about

Real advice from people who get outside.

We carry the labels we actually reach for ourselves — from technical shells and merino base layers to polarized optics and insulated bottles. If it's on our shelves, it's because we'd use it.

Stop in and we'll help you find the right fit for whatever's next: a first 5k, a winter on the mountain, a summer on the water, or just a better layer for the dog walk.
